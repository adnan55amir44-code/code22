How to Use Dowebcode Learn & Earn

1. Create your account

Enter your name, phone, email and a secure password.

2. Verify email

Enter the six-digit OTP sent to your email.

3. Get your Member ID

Your permanent DWC Member ID is created after verification.

4. Watch your lesson

Play the assigned YouTube lesson and build valid watch progress.

5. Reach the watch threshold

The platform checks meaningful playback progress instead of trusting a finish button.

6. Tell us what you learned

Write at least the configured number of characters in your own words.

7. Speak to Write

Use your browser's built-in speech recognition where supported. No speech API key is required.

8. Earn your coin

After backend validation, attendance is recorded and your daily coin is issued.

9. Unlock rewards

Complete the configured target to unlock website source code and/or prompts.

10. My ID

Your digital member card can be printed using the browser print dialog.

11. Ads

Advertisements are informational/monetization content only. Ad clicks never earn coins.

12. Privacy

Your learning responses are kept inside your account records and are not public.
